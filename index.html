Seaworthy

Boat maintenance for iPhone

Seaworthy keeps track of maintenance for your sail and motor boats: what is due, what is overdue, and what you did last time.

  • Maintenance plan built from what is on board: engines, drives, rig, water, gas, safety gear and more.
  • Due by months, engine hours or season — whichever comes first.
  • Manufacturer intervals for common engines and saildrives, and country rules for your home waters.
  • Log work with date, engine hours, cost and notes.
  • Private by design: your data stays on your iPhone. No account, no tracking.
