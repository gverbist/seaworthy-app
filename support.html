Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

Why are my tasks orange?
A new boat's tasks have no starting point yet. Tell Seaworthy when each job was last done (or start the clock today) via the orange "Set up" row on the Today tab.

How are the intervals chosen?
If you pick your engine or saildrive model, Seaworthy uses the manufacturer's published intervals. Your home waters add country rules (for example gas installation checks). Everything else uses typical industry intervals. Open a task to see what its interval is based on and choose another option, or set your own.

Can I change an interval?
Yes. Open the task and change it under Settings. Intervals you set yourself are never overwritten.

How do I delete a boat?
Boats → open the boat → Edit → Delete boat. This removes the boat and all its data from your iPhone.
